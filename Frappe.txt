Frappe Commands:

Bench Directory(frappe-bench) Initialization:
    bench init frappe-bench

App installation in Frappe:
    bench new-app appname

Site installation in Frappe:
    bench new-site sitename

Site uninstallation in Frappe:
    bench drop-site sitename

Adding the app in the site:
    bench --site sitename install-app appname

Removing the app from the site:
    bench --site sitename uninstall-app appname

Make the Site as the default current site:
    bench use sitename

Migrate the site:
    bench --site sitename migrate
